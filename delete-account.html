Account & data deletion

Request removal of your Fishito account and associated game data. You can contact us even if you’ve already uninstalled the app.

Request account deletion

Email lephron.studios@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Fishito account. Tell us whether you want the entire Fishito account removed or have a more specific data request.

Start a deletion request

This opens an email draft. Nothing is submitted until you send it. You can also copy the email address into any mail service.

What to include

  • Whether you played on Android or iOS.
  • Your Fishito username, if you know it.
  • Whether you still have access to the game and its linked platform account.

We’ll reply with any additional information needed to locate the correct account and verify ownership. A username alone is not proof of ownership. Do not send passwords, verification codes, platform access tokens, or full payment details.

A manual request, with confirmation

Sending an email starts a request; it does not immediately erase data. We verify the account before processing and confirm the outcome by email. Deleting Fishito account data removes progress and may prevent restoration of consumed gems or equipment.

What is deleted

  • Your Fishito cloud save, including progression, currencies, upgrades, equipment, fish collection, and recorded depth.
  • Your Fishito profile and username, daily-reward record, and purchase-delivery ledger.
  • Your entries in Fishito’s depth, collection, and level leaderboards.
  • The Unity Authentication account associated with that Fishito player.

What requires a separate step

  • Local device data: remove Fishito’s app data or uninstall the game on your devices. A cloud-deletion request cannot remotely erase an offline device. Reconnecting an old local save can create fresh online records, so remove local copies if you want them gone.
  • Analytics: use Settings → GAME ANALYTICS → DELETE ANALYTICS DATA and keep sharing off while deletion completes. If you can no longer open the app, include this in your email so we can determine the available provider process.
  • Google or Apple accounts: we delete the Fishito game account, not your Google Play Games, Google, Game Center, or Apple account.
  • Store payment records: Google Play and Apple retain their own transaction records under their policies. Account deletion does not automatically request a refund.
  • Advertising-provider data: use the provider’s privacy process and device controls; let us know if you need help finding the relevant process.

Retention after the request

We remove the Fishito account data listed above after ownership is verified. Request correspondence may be kept for up to 90 days after resolution, then deleted, unless legally required for longer. If a legal obligation requires other records to be retained, we explain what is retained, why, and for how long. Records held independently by Google, Apple, or another provider follow that provider’s retention policy.

For more details, read the Fishito privacy policy. For a gameplay issue instead, visit player support.
