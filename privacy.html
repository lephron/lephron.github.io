Who we are

Lephron Games develops Fishito, a mobile fishing and collection game. Our public support and privacy contact is lephron.studios@gmail.com. Features such as ads and purchases may be unavailable in some versions; the descriptions below apply when those features are enabled.

Information we handle and why

Gameplay progress and device preferences

Fishito stores progress on your device: level and XP, coins and gems, upgrade levels, fish discoveries, equipment inventory, daily reward progress, purchase-history revision, and deepest depth reached. Device preferences include music, vibration, controls, refresh rate, and analytics choice. We use these to run the game, preserve your progress, and apply your choices.

Platform sign-in and cloud saves

On Android, Fishito can authenticate through Google Play Games; on iOS, through Apple Game Center. Unity Authentication creates or identifies your Fishito player account using the platform sign-in. We process platform player identifiers, authentication proof, and a Unity player identifier to identify your account and provide cloud backup and restoration. Profile names and platform avatar references are used for account presentation. Fishito does not ask you to give us your platform password.

Cloud backups contain gameplay progress and synchronization metadata, such as account and snapshot identifiers. Server records also hold your Fishito username, daily-claim history, and purchase-delivery history. These support restoration, prevent duplicate grants, and handle conflicting saves.

Public leaderboard information

When signed-in progress synchronizes, Fishito may publish your deepest reached depth, discovered-fish count, and level to the game’s leaderboards. Other players can see your Fishito username, rank, scores, and supported platform avatar. Leaderboard services also process player identifiers and avatar references to retrieve those entries. Choose a username that does not disclose private information.

Optional gameplay analytics

Gameplay sharing starts off. If you turn on Settings → GAME ANALYTICS → Share gameplay data, Unity Analytics receives events about screens, trips, depth, catches by rarity, discoveries, rewards, upgrades, equipment, daily claims, rewarded-ad actions, and purchase delivery. Events include game level, event schema, and, where relevant, a random trip identifier.

Our custom events exclude usernames, platform account IDs, receipts, access tokens, and complete save contents. Unity’s SDK separately processes session, device, app, network, and identifier information under its privacy documentation. Analytics helps us understand gameplay and improve the game; it is not needed to play.

Rewarded ads

Where enabled, Google AdMob provides optional rewarded ads. Its SDK may process IP address (including approximate location derived from it), device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Processing depends on your device, region, permissions, and consent choices. We use Google’s consent tools to present applicable choices. If tracking authorization is used on iOS, the platform permission applies. We do not send your Fishito save contents to AdMob.

Purchases

If gem purchases are available, Google Play or Apple processes payment. We receive product and transaction references or purchase proofs needed to validate the order and deliver gems. The server checks the store’s transaction information and retains a hashed receipt reference and delivery history to avoid duplicate grants. The store receives a derived account-binding identifier. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or details you choose to send. We use them to answer you, investigate the issue, or handle a privacy request. Our contact inbox is hosted by Google’s email service.

Service providers and disclosure

Information is processed by providers needed for the features you use:

  • Unity: Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Leaderboards, and optional Analytics.
  • Google: Play Games sign-in, Play purchase processing/verification, AdMob and its advertising partners where ads are enabled, and our email inbox.
  • Apple: Game Center sign-in, platform photos, and App Store purchase processing/verification.
  • GitHub: hosting this website.

Provider infrastructure may process information in countries other than yours. Their own privacy terms apply to their services. We may also disclose information where necessary to comply with applicable law, protect users, investigate fraud, or respond to valid legal requests.

Your choices and privacy requests

  • You can continue fishing locally if platform sign-in or cloud services are unavailable. Online daily claims and other account features need authentication and connectivity.
  • You can turn gameplay sharing off in Settings. No pre-consent gameplay is replayed when you enable sharing.
  • You can request analytics-data deletion using Settings → GAME ANALYTICS → DELETE ANALYTICS DATA. Keep sharing off while the request completes. This does not delete your cloud save or platform account.
  • Where available, use ADS PRIVACY in Settings and your device’s privacy controls to manage ad-related choices.
  • You can contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your Fishito account. Additional rights may apply under your local law.

We may need reasonable information to locate your records and verify ownership before acting on an account request. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, or payment-card details.

Retention, security, and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until removed by you or the operating system. Cloud account data remains available for the game’s account features until you request deletion. Uninstalling the app does not by itself remove a cloud account or online rankings.

Resolved support and deletion-request correspondence is retained for up to 90 days after resolution, then deleted, unless a longer period is legally required. For a verified account-deletion request, we remove the associated Fishito cloud progress, profile, daily-reward record, purchase-delivery ledger, leaderboard entries, and Unity Authentication account. Store-held payment records and your Google or Apple account are controlled by those providers; their retention rules apply separately. If law requires us to retain a record, we explain the category, reason, and applicable retention period in our response.

The account and data deletion page explains how to make the request and what local data you may need to remove. Analytics deletion uses the separate in-game SDK process described above; contact us if you can no longer access it.

We use access controls, encrypted network connections, and platform-supported local-save integrity checks. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

Children and families

Parents or guardians with questions about a child’s information can contact us. If you believe a child provided personal information that should be removed, tell us so we can investigate and handle the request. Age eligibility and any family-related conditions are shown in the relevant store listing and platform terms.

This website

This static site does not include our own analytics scripts, ad widgets, tracking cookies, login, or online submission forms. GitHub Pages processes visitor IP addresses and technical request information for hosting and security. Selecting an email link opens your email application; your message is sent only when you choose to send it.

Contact and policy updates

For privacy questions, email lephron.studios@gmail.com. We update this policy when our practices change and show the revised date above.
