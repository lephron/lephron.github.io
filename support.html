Help us understand the issue

  • Your device model, Android or iOS version, and Fishito app version.
  • What happened, what you expected, and any steps that reproduce the issue.
  • Your Fishito username and whether you use Google Play Games or Apple Game Center, if the question concerns saved progress.
  • An optional screenshot with private information removed.

Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, access tokens, complete save files, or payment-card information.

Before you write

My cloud progress is missing.

Check your internet connection and sign in with the same Google Play Games or Game Center account used before. Return to the Main Menu and open the cloud-save panel through Settings. If two saves are shown, check both before choosing. Avoid reinstalling or resetting while your progress is only saved locally.

Can I move progress from Android to iPhone?

Cloud restoration currently works within the same platform account. Android-to-iOS progress transfer is not supported.

A daily reward is unavailable.

Daily claims require internet, platform sign-in, and synchronized cloud progress. The next reward becomes available on the next server UTC calendar day. Changing the device clock does not unlock another claim.

I paid for gems but they haven’t appeared.

If purchases are available in your version, check the store payment status and reconnect using the same game account. A pending payment does not grant gems until approved. If the issue continues, email your store order reference, product name, platform, and Fishito username. Remove unrelated information from any receipt screenshot. Do not send purchase tokens or full payment details.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled under the applicable Google Play or Apple App Store process. See Google Play refund help or Apple refund help. Contact us if you need help with a Fishito delivery issue.

How do I change analytics or ad privacy settings?

In Settings, GAME ANALYTICS lets you turn gameplay sharing off or request analytics-data deletion. Sharing starts off. ADS PRIVACY appears when the ad provider requires a privacy-options entry point. These choices are separate from platform account and cloud-save deletion.
